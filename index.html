Camp Woofs and Wiggles
is your dog's home away from home while you are at work or on vacation.
Our trained staff will ensure that your furry family member remains under a watchful eye and will have all of their needs met whether they are here for a day or a week.

Camp Woofs and Wiggles
offers a variety of indoor and outdoor spaces for your dog to run, play, and adventure with their new-found friends.
Additional services include training, bathing/grooming, and pup parties- hosted birthday parties for your pup and their friends!
